WBot Assistant uses Qt for Python (PySide6 and Shiboken) 6.12.0.

The installed PySide6-Essentials distribution declares:
LGPL-3.0-only OR GPL-2.0-only OR GPL-3.0-only.

Qt is loaded as separate shared libraries under _internal/PySide6 and
_internal/shiboken6. The upstream distribution license notices are retained
alongside the GNU license texts in the portable package's licenses directory.
